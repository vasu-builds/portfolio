Our Story

Born from fire, shaped by seasons

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief — that the best meals are cooked slowly, with wood and fire, using ingredients pulled from the earth just days before they reach your plate.

Our wood-burning hearth is the heart of the restaurant. Every dish passes through its warmth. The smoke, the char, the caramelized edges — these aren't accidents. They're the point.

We work with a small network of farmers within 100 miles of our kitchen, changing the menu with the seasons, honoring what's actually ready to be eaten.
